GND in real life

  • Drawn as one frame so you can see everything that needs it.
  • Built as your pigtail: one wire from the ESP32 GND socket to a splice, then a lead to each module, the roof harness and the two capacitors.

Parts and where they sit

  • R1, R2 4.7k (RTC pull-ups), C1 100µF (EN, upload fix): on the carrier board.
  • C2 100µF: on the carrier, next to the STRIP pins.
  • R4 330Ω and D1 1N4007: in-line in the strip wires (stripe toward the strip).
  • 3 × 220Ω: at the status LEDs, under the roof.

Watch out

  • Display and RTC VCC are 5V; the knob's + is 3V3.
  • Display RST goes to the ESP32's EN pin, not a G pin.
  • Grey ESP32 pins are not used. G35 is free: the Play/Pause button was dropped (play/pause moves to the knob later).
